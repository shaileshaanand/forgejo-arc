Create a same-namespace Secret with a Forgejo API token, then apply a RunnerPool.
See config/samples/ and README.md in the forgejo-arc source repository.

Watch pools:
  kubectl get runnerpools -A

Delete RunnerPools and wait for their finalizers before uninstalling the controller.
Keep their credentials Secrets available until deletion completes.

CRD upgrades are explicit:
  kubectl apply -f charts/forgejo-arc/crds/
